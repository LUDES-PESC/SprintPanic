👥 Responsabilidades Scrum

🧭 Product OwnerMaximizes value, communicates the Product Goal, and effectively manages the Product Backlog. Does not assign tasks to Developers.
🛠️ DevelopersSelect work in Planning, create and adapt the Sprint Backlog, conduct the Daily Scrum, and deliver Increments that meet the Definition of Done.
🧩 Scrum MasterHelps Scrum be understood and effective, works on impediments, and promotes improvement. Is not the team manager and does not chair the Daily Scrum.

🗓️ Eventos representados

Sprint PlanningDefines why the Sprint is valuable, what can be done, and how the work will begin.
Daily Scrum15 minutes in reality; here it is compressed into one decision. Developers inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan.
Sprint ReviewThe Scrum Team and stakeholders inspect the Sprint outcome and progress toward the Product Goal; the Product Backlog may be adapted.
RetrospectiveThe Scrum Team plans ways to increase quality and effectiveness.

🚦 Fluxo, WIP e Swarming

WIP (Work in Progress)The game suggests at most 2 items in progress at once. With 3 or more, flow efficiency drops because of context switching and coordination. When an item finishes, the team automatically pulls the next priority work to avoid idle capacity.
SwarmingTwo or three Developers can collaborate on the same item. This speeds up that item, but with diminishing returns.
VelocityCounts only product PBI points that reach Done. Emergent technical work consumes capacity without inflating velocity.

📏 Indicadores do jogo

Stakeholder trustRepresents how predictable and reliable customers and stakeholders perceive the product and team to be.
Technical debtRepresents future work created by technical shortcuts.
